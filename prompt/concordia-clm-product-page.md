## Content

### Opening (before any headings)

ConcordiaCLM gives law firms and in-house legal teams one place to manage every
agreement — from first draft to final closure — with the governance standards that
clients and auditors expect.

It runs on your existing Windows infrastructure. No cloud subscription. No data
leaving your premises.

### The Problem

Contract management failures are not dramatic. They are quiet, cumulative, and
expensive. The renewal date that nobody caught. The version that went to the client
after two rounds of revisions had been quietly overwritten. The audit request that
required three days of inbox archaeology to answer.

Specific failure modes this replaces:

- Renewal dates, statutory filings, and board approvals tracked across spreadsheets,
  emails, and physical files — no single source of truth
- Multiple versions of the same contract circulating with no clear record of who
  approved what, and when
- Proving compliance to auditors requires reconstructing a timeline from scattered
  records
- Expired contracts that should have renewed, delayed payments, and missed notice
  periods — real revenue lost to poor tracking

### What ConcordiaCLM Delivers

**Never miss a deadline again** — Every contract milestone is tracked and flagged
automatically: payment dates, renewal windows, regulatory filings, board approvals.
Overdue items appear in red, approaching deadlines in amber. Nothing slips because
nobody had it in their diary.

**A complete record of every agreement** — From first draft to signed version,
ConcordiaCLM keeps the full history of each contract: who made changes, who approved
them, and when. A clean audit trail that holds up under scrutiny.

**Governance built in, not bolted on** — Designed around CGI and ICSA governance
standards, with fields for governing law, jurisdiction, and regulatory references
specific to Ugandan legal requirements. Compliance is part of the workflow, not an
afterthought.

**Know your portfolio at a glance** — Three purpose-built dashboards give management,
legal teams, and compliance officers live visibility into the full contract portfolio:
active agreements, total value, upcoming renewals, and risk indicators. No manual
report required.

**All parties, all relationships, one place** — Every counterparty — individuals,
companies, government bodies — tracked with full contact details, registration
numbers, and the contracts that link you to them. No hunting across systems.

**Runs on your existing hardware** — ConcordiaCLM is a Windows desktop application.
Installs on the computers your team already uses, connects to your existing database
server. Your IT team or a consultant sets it up in a single day.

### How Deployment Works

Three steps:

**Install and configure** — ConcordiaCLM installs on your Windows computers and
connects to your existing database server. Setup typically takes one day.

**Bring in your contracts** — Add your active agreements, counterparties, and upcoming
deadlines. The system generates clean reference numbers and organises everything
automatically.

**Manage with confidence** — From day one, your team has full visibility, timely
alerts, and a compliance-ready audit trail.

### Who It's For

**Law firms** — from boutique practices to multi-partner firms managing client
contracts, retainers, and service agreements.

**In-house legal teams** — corporate and government legal departments managing a high
volume of supplier, employment, and service contracts.

**Compliance and governance officers** — directors and compliance leads who need
instant evidence of governance adherence for board reporting, regulatory submissions,
and client audits.

**Finance and executive leadership** — executives who need real-time visibility into
contract portfolio value, exposure, and upcoming obligations without waiting for a
manual summary.

### Governance Standards

ConcordiaCLM is designed specifically for the governance standards applicable to
Ugandan legal practice:

- **CGI Compliant** — Chartered Governance Institute standards for board-level
  contract governance
- **ICSA Standards** — Institute of Chartered Secretaries and Administrators
  governance workflow requirements
- **Uganda Law** — Jurisdiction fields, governing law references, and regulatory
  citations aligned to Ugandan legal frameworks
- **Full Audit Trail** — Every change, approval, and notification logged with
  timestamp and user attribution

### Contact and Next Steps

ConcordiaCLM is deployed, not self-served. A setup session covers your contract
taxonomy, your counterparty structure, and your governance workflow. Most deployments
are operational within a day.
